Zephyr Coffee Co. Brewing Guide

Water and Ratio
For the best flavor, use water heated to 93 degrees Celsius. Use a ratio of 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water. For a standard 250 ml mug, that is about 15 grams of coffee.

Grind Size
Use a medium-coarse grind for French press, with a 4 minute steep. Use a fine grind for espresso. Grinding just before brewing keeps the aroma strongest.

Storage
Store beans in an airtight container away from light and heat. Do not keep beans in the refrigerator, because moisture can ruin the flavor. Use opened bags within 4 weeks.
