Zephyr Coffee Co. Customer Handbook

Returns and Refunds
Customers may return unopened coffee bags within 45 days of purchase for a full refund. Opened bags can be exchanged within 14 days if the coffee does not taste as described. Equipment such as grinders and brewers has a 90-day warranty.

Shipping
Orders over $40 ship free within the continental United States. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs $12 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. We do not ship internationally at this time.

Subscriptions
Subscribers save 10 percent on every order. A subscription can be paused for up to 3 months, or cancelled at any time before the 5th of the month, when the next order is processed.

Roasting
All beans are roasted in small batches every Tuesday and Thursday. Beans are shipped within 24 hours of roasting to preserve freshness.
